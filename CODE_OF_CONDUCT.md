# Code of Conduct

PayForWhat should be a place where people can solve practical problems and contribute
without intimidation.

Participants must:

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes rather than people.
- Make room for different ages, languages, abilities, backgrounds, and levels of
  experience.
- Respect privacy and never expose personal information.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, spam, or
  sustained disruption.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Project spaces include repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, and events or
messages made on behalf of the project.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[private report form](https://github.com/m0p4rk/PayForWhat/security/advisories/new) and
prefix the title with `Conduct:`. This use of the confidential channel does not imply
that the report is a software vulnerability. Do not include sensitive personal details
in a public issue. The lead maintainer may warn, restrict, remove, or ban a participant
and may remove content that violates these expectations.

Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately, and kept
confidential to the extent practical. Retaliation against a good-faith report is
prohibited.
