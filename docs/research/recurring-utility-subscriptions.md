Research date: 2026-08-14

This is independent factual market research. PayForWhat is not affiliated with,

The catalog summarizes publicly available pricing, help, and terms pages in original

## Conclusion

Recurring billing is common far beyond PDF software. This review found **85 distinct web
services with a recurring plan** for tasks that may be completed once or only
occasionally.

Many of the reviewed tasks do not require permanent hosting. They are local or
short-lived operations such as exporting a resume, transforming a file, exporting
without a free-tier watermark, generating a static QR code, resizing an image,
formatting text, or showing the steps of a calculation.

This inventory does not claim that every subscription is unnecessary, deceptive, or
widely purchased. A public pricing page proves that a recurring offer exists, not how
many people pay for it or why. Many services also provide free tiers, trials,
pay-as-you-go credits, or one-time purchases. The product hypothesis examined here is
whether a recurring model should be the default answer for low-frequency tasks.

## Method

- Official pricing pages, help centers, and terms were used wherever available.
- Prices are snapshots and may vary by country, currency, tax, platform, promotion, or
  checkout experiment.
- `Recurring only` means no one-time option was found for the relevant paid result.
- `Recurring with alternative` means an official one-time, lifetime, day-pass,
  week-pass, or pay-as-you-go option exists.
- `Limited free tier` means the service can be used indefinitely for free, but a
  material output, quota, quality level, or export is restricted.

## Repeated market patterns

1. **Some plans bill every four weeks rather than monthly**

   A 28-day cycle can produce 13 billing dates within a 365-day period.

2. **Some services restrict export by plan**

   Certain free tiers allow creation or editing while restricting PDF, Word, HD, or
   watermark-free output.

3. **A short trial becomes recurring billing**

   This appears across document, image, resume, invitation, fax, writing, and
   AI-detection products.

4. **Monthly credits may reset or expire**

   Some subscription credits reset or expire at the end of a billing period.

5. **Hosted or dynamic output may depend on an active plan**

   Dynamic QR redirects, hosted screenshots, short links, stored secrets, and other
   persistent outputs can depend on an active plan.

6. **Pay-per-use alternatives exist in some categories**

   Sejda, PDF Candy, Online-Convert, CloudConvert, Upscale.media, RouteXL, Greetings
   Island, Looka, and others demonstrate day passes, week passes, lifetime purchases, or
   one-time credits.

7. **The same behavior is fragmented across many verticals**

   No single category defines the opportunity. The common unit is a low-frequency
   digital task.

## Product opportunity for PayForWhat

### Direct fit: browser-first and low marginal cost

- PDF merge, split, reorder, rotate, crop, basic compression, and image-to-PDF
- Image resize, crop, compression, metadata removal, and format conversion
- Static QR codes, barcodes, and simple generators
- Text cleanup, diffing, formatting, counting, and deterministic conversion
- Resume and cover-letter composition with unrestricted PDF export
- Invitation, card, label, and basic template export
- Citation formatting and deterministic educational calculators
- Audio trim, format conversion, channel operations, and compression
- Video trim, resize, mute, format conversion, and compression on capable devices

### Conditional fit: local models or controlled server cost

- Background removal and object cleanup
- Image upscaling
- OCR and document extraction
- Speech-to-text and subtitle generation
- Symbolic math explanations beyond deterministic solvers
- Office-document conversion and repair

### Poor initial fit: genuine persistent cost or legal responsibility

- Fax transmission
- Dynamic QR redirects and short-link hosting
- Recipient-facing electronic signature with identity and audit trails
- Password storage
- Bank synchronization and financial aggregation
- Continuous webpage capture and archival
- High-volume route optimization

## Positioning implication

PayForWhat should not present itself as a PDF application. It is an **open utility
commons for low-frequency digital tasks**.

The defensible public statement is:

> A five-minute task should not become another subscription. PayForWhat is designed to
> keep its core tools free, open source, and local-first whenever the work can be done
> safely on your device.

The statement should target the recurring-payment pattern rather than accuse a named
company of malicious intent.
